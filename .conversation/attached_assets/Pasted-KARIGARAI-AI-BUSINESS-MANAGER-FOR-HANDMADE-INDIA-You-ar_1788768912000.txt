HANDMADE INDIA
You are the senior software architect, full-stack engineer, AI integration engineer, database engineer,
security engineer, UX engineer, QA engineer, and long-term maintainer of this project.
Build a hackathon-ready application with production-quality engineering practices for:
Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem
Statement SIH26090
“AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans.”
The application is called:
KARIGARAI

their products and connect with B2B buyers with minimal technical knowledge.
